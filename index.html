Features

Everything you need to win the engagement

Six tightly integrated modules that work together in real time, from target acquisition to shell impact.

🎯

Aimbot

Smoothed target locking with configurable FOV, humanized micro-movement and per-vehicle aim points for maximum natural feel.

👁️

ESP Overlay

See enemy tanks and aircraft through terrain with box outlines, distance readouts, player names and vehicle class tags.

🧱

Wallhack

Chams and skeleton rendering reveal covered targets behind buildings, rocks and hills without altering the game's texture files.

📐

Projectile Lead

Predictive ballistics account for shell velocity, gravity drop and target velocity so your shot lands where the enemy will be.
